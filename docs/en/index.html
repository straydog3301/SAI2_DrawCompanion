DrawCompanion sits quietly next to PaintTool SAI2. It records how long you spend on each piece, plans your daily practice, and keeps gesture-drawing and study references in a small window that never covers your canvas.

Tracks time on its own

Detects which file you're drawing in SAI2 and counts only the time you're actually making strokes. No start button, nothing to forget to stop.

Know what to practice today

Gesture drawing, master studies and drills planned around the skills you want to improve. Check in when you're done and watch your streak and skills grow.

Stays out of your way

Most of the time it's just a small pet. References open in a click-through window, so you can look and draw even on a single monitor.

Rewritten from 1.x. None of this existed before.

Features

Real 2.0 screens with sample data, shown with the Chinese interface. The app itself also comes in English and Japanese.

DESKTOP PET × QUICK PANEL

Click Momo and everything you use is right there

After launch, only Momo appears on your desktop. Drag it anywhere. Click it to open the quick panel:

  • Today's progress: minutes drawn today and your streak
  • Daily tasks: start, continue, check in
  • Recent: artwork thumbnails that open straight in SAI2
  • Shortcut buttons: gesture drawing, studies, snapshots, timelapse… and you choose which

Momo reacts to what's happening in SAI2 and reminds you to take a break when you've been drawing too long. The session time and recording toggle sit right under the pet.

REFERENCE WINDOW

Gesture drawing and studies in a small window

  • Gesture drawing: images rotate from your own folders or online libraries, and the timer only runs while you draw. It pauses while you look at the reference
  • Canvas created for you: each round opens a fresh A4 canvas in SAI2
  • Studies: 7 learning paths, each lesson with steps, key points and a completion check. You can also add your own or community courses
  • Click-through mode: a semi-transparent window your mouse passes through, laid over the canvas to draw along
  • Hold to compare: hold a hotkey to overlay the reference on your canvas
  • AI review (optional): scores and short feedback on check-in using your own AI key, to decide whether you move to the next lesson (Google AI Studio recommended; it has a free daily quota)
  • Grayscale, value steps, composition guides and other checking tools
FLOATING NOTES × REFERENCE VIEWER

Keep your settings and references at hand

  • Floating notes: multiple tabs for color swatches, brush settings, character sheets. Anything wrapped in ``` copies with one click
  • Read and edit modes; save with "Done" or Ctrl+S. Headings collapse, and a table of contents jumps between them
  • Paste screenshots with Ctrl+V while editing, click an image to enlarge or copy it back, and pin the window on top
  • Reference viewer: open any reference image outside a course (open, paste or drag in), zoom and pan, use the eyedropper, grayscale, composition guides, click-through and hold to compare
TODAY

See what to practice at a glance

The Today page sums up today's drawing time, daily tasks, this week's chart and skill activity, and points out skills you haven't practiced in a while.

  • Tasks are planned from your daily practice time and the skills you want to improve. Swap one or add your own
  • Take days off each week without breaking your streak
  • A 30-second setup on first launch, and a suggestion after 3 days based on how much you actually drew
GROWTH

Every day of effort, visible

  • Practice calendar: darker colors mean more check-ins. Click a day to see what you did
  • Drawing time stats: week, month, six months, year
  • Skill cards: hours and level for each skill
  • A canvas snapshot is saved on every check-in so you can look back at your progress
WORKS

How long each piece took, with thumbnails

  • Thumbnails captured automatically when you save or pause, right in the list
  • Drawing time, open time, efficiency, and number of sessions
  • Skill tags: which skills this piece's time counts toward
  • Merge two records after "Save as". Timelapse videos live here too
MAKE IT YOURS

Your characters, your rules

  • Custom characters: use your own art, with a different animation for each state (PNG / GIF / APNG / WebP)
  • Up to 5 pets in rotation, custom actions (wave, stretch…), and automatic sprite-sheet slicing
  • Speech bubble skins: use any image as the bubble and mark the text area; corners never stretch. Each pet can have its own
  • "If… then…" rules: e.g. "50 minutes of drawing → Momo reminds you to rest and does an action", or "drag Momo → play an action the whole way and say something when dropped". Rules can be per pet
  • Theme colors and light/dark mode, quick panel sections and buttons, hotkeys
  • Text size from 75% to 150%
  • Export and share settings and characters, publish characters to the Community Pet Library, or add other people's with one click

Get started

No installer. Three steps after unzipping

Download and unzip

Download DrawCompanion2.zip (about 36 MB), right-click it → "Extract All" into a folder such as D:\DrawCompanion.

Keep the files together

The folder contains DrawCompanion2.exe and an _internal folder; keep them side by side. Your records, settings, thumbnails and videos are stored here too, so move the whole folder if you relocate it.

Double-click DrawCompanion2.exe

When Momo appears on your desktop, you're set. Open SAI2 and start drawing. Everything else has sensible defaults and will prompt you when needed.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • PaintTool SAI Ver.2
  • Online image libraries (Unsplash / Pexels) need a free API key; local folders work without one

On first launch

  • Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click "More info → Run anyway" (details)
  • Unzip first; don't run it from inside the zip, and don't move the exe out on its own
  • Only one copy runs at a time; opening it again won't start a second one

"Windows protected your PC" on first launch?

That's normal. Two clicks and you're through, and it won't appear again.

Why it appears

This window is Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, built into Windows. It holds back programs that are new, have few downloads and aren't code-signed. DrawCompanion is made by one person and doesn't carry a code-signing certificate (which costs hundreds of dollars a year), so Windows doesn't "know" it yet. It is not a virus warning; the window doesn't report any threat.

Why you can trust it

  • Downloads are hosted on GitHub Releases, and the built-in updater verifies the developer's signature before installing anything
  • It reads SAI2's window title to see which file you're drawing; it never changes SAI2's settings or your existing files
  • No administrator rights needed; records stay in the program's own folder
  • Please only download from the button on this page
  • Still unsure? Upload the zip to VirusTotal to scan it with dozens of antivirus engines

Alternative: before unzipping, right-click the zip → "Properties" → tick "Unblock" at the bottom → OK. The extracted program won't show the warning. On work or school PCs without a "Run anyway" button, ask your administrator.

FAQ

Is the app available in English?

Yes. The app and the menus and dialogs it adds to SAI2 are available in English, Japanese and Traditional Chinese. It follows your Windows language by default; you can change it under Settings › General › Language (takes effect after a restart).

Does it work with Photoshop or Clip Studio Paint?

Yes, since 2.2. Switch in "Settings › General › Drawing app" and drawing time, check-ins, artwork records and canvas captures follow that app. Photoshop captures need the window to be unobstructed; with several Clip Studio Paint canvases open, the most recently opened one counts, and captures show the last saved state. Timelapse and Mods are SAI2-only for now.

Why a zip instead of a single exe?

The folder version doesn't unpack itself to a temp folder on every launch, so it starts faster and is less likely to be flagged by antivirus software. The updater also replaces this folder directly. Run DrawCompanion2.exe and keep the _internal folder next to it.

What if an update breaks something?

If a new version fails to start three times in a row, the app rolls back automatically. You can also click "Go back to previous version" in "Settings › General › Updates". The previous version is kept in the _previous folder.

Do I need Python or anything else?

No. The unzipped folder contains everything; just run DrawCompanion2.exe. Timelapse videos are made with a feature built into Windows, so you don't need ffmpeg either.

Is my artwork uploaded anywhere?

No. Records, thumbnails and settings stay on your computer, in the program's folder. The app only connects to fetch reference images (The Met, Art Institute of Chicago, Unsplash, Pexels), check for updates, browse the community libraries, activate a license, and for AI review if you turn it on. Only courses and characters you choose to publish are uploaded; no usage data is collected.

Why isn't the gesture-drawing timer moving?

By default the timer only runs while you draw on a saved canvas in SAI2, and pauses while you look at the reference. A canvas is created and opened for you when you start. To keep it running all the time, turn this off in the reference window's settings.

The pet is in my way. What can I do?

Drag Momo anywhere; it also turns semi-transparent while you draw. "Hide for 30 min" in the quick panel tucks it away, and the tray icon brings it back.

Can I use my own character?

Yes. Go to "Settings → Companions", click "+" in the lineup, and pick images for each state. 512×512 APNG or WebP with a transparent background works best, and sprite sheets are supported. Export character packs to share, publish them to the Community Pet Library, or add characters others have drawn.
